About us

We are FourHub

FourHub is our web portfolio. It puts our team, our resumes, and our work in one simple place.

Our goal

To show our team and our work clearly, so anyone who visits can quickly find out who we are and what each of us does.

How we work

Every member has a role. We plan together, share ideas, and help each other so the work is finished on time.

What we use

This website is built with only HTML and CSS, in orange and black, so it loads fast and works on any phone or computer.
